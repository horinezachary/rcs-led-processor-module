Brompton walls

The Topology page draws every Brompton wall from its processors' cable loops without any setup. Tessera does not say how many tiles are on each chain, so to show tiles, describe the wall here: the processor pair, and for each pair of XD boxes the tiles on each port's chain. A chain runs from port N on the left box to port N on the right box as a cable loop. NovaStar COEX walls come from the processor and need nothing here.

"Fill in from" starts from the XD pairs and cabled ports in the processor's last cable loop report, without asking the processor anything. Tessera does not say how many tiles are on each chain, so new chains share its tile count evenly: check each one.
